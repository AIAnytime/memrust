# Startup Ecosystem Intelligence Skill

## Objective

Build and continuously maintain the world's highest quality database of startup accelerators, incubators, fellowships, venture studios, startup programs, innovation hubs, AI infrastructure programs, and founder communities.

The primary focus is:

1. AI startups
2. AI infrastructure startups
3. Deep Tech
4. Developer tools
5. Enterprise AI
6. Agentic AI
7. Open-source AI
8. Robotics
9. Foundation Models
10. Research-driven startups

The final deliverable must be a clean Excel workbook suitable for founder research and fundraising.

---

# Mission

Do not simply scrape lists.

Research every program deeply.

Verify every field.

Prioritize official sources.

Flag outdated information.

Identify application windows.

Identify which founders should apply.

The output should become the canonical internal database.
